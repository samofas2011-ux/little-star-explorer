Your adventure starts here ✧

  1. Drag the sky with one finger to look around.
  2. Pinch with two fingers or use + and − to zoom.
  3. Tap a star to find its name and color.
  4. Choose a constellation and follow its connect-the-dots shape.
  5. Try Read to me to hear the words aloud.

This is a map of the whole sky, not a live view from your garden. Stars can be very far apart even when they look close together. The drawings are one way people imagine the patterns; different cultures tell different stories.

Your discoveries are saved on this device when browser storage is available. No account needed in a copy hosted on your own website.
